# MYNO Agent Rules

Before modifying this repository:

1. Read `MYNO_PROJECT_MEMORY.md`.
2. Determine the current project phase.
3. Follow its architectural invariants.
4. Do not skip roadmap gates.
5. Do not introduce unrelated features.
6. Preserve unrelated working-tree changes.
7. Never claim implementation/certification without evidence.
8. Update `MYNO_PROJECT_MEMORY.md` when a major architectural
   decision or project-state milestone changes.
